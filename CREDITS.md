## What is actually from Bihar

- `biharr.jpg` is the only photograph here taken in Bihar: foxnuts being lightly roasted in
  Nawada district, supplied under CC BY-SA 4.0.
- `plant-howrah-*.jpg` and `plant-nandankanan.jpg` show the makhana plant (*Euryale ferox*)
  in India — Howrah (West Bengal) and Nandankanan (Odisha) — not in Bihar. They are labelled
  with their real location in the captions.
- The remaining images are makhana (fox nuts) as a product and as food: roasted, spiced and
  in kheer. They are representative, not photographs of Shagun Enterprises lots.
